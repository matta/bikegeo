## Gathered Drawing Specifications

- **Model / Wheel / Axle:** MY26 FOX 34 SL, 29", `15QRx110` Boost (`110 mm` spacing)
- **Steerer & crown interface:** 1.5" Tapered (`Ø28.58 ±0.05 mm` upper, `Ø39.808 ±0.030 mm` crown race seat, `244 ±5 mm` uncut length; drawing notes `Ø58 mm` head tube crowns only)
- **Offset / Rake option:** `44 ±2.5 mm` only (`51 mm` is not offered on MY26 34 SL)
- **Axle-to-crown by travel:**
  - `110 mm` travel: `520.5 mm` extended (not included in catalog; `Q129` closes Slice 16c travel to `{100, 120, 130}`)
  - `120 mm` travel: `530.5 mm` extended, `410.5 mm` compressed
  - `130 mm` travel: `540.5 mm` extended, `410.5 mm` compressed
- **Max tire clearance:** `29 × 2.50"` (`755 mm` peak diameter / `64.5 mm` max width)
