## Component specs

**ENVE Mountain Fork** (S5; the fork the frame shipped with in 2022, per S4):

| Version | Offset | Axle to crown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boost 15 x 110mm | 44mm | 490mm |
| Boost 15 x 110mm | 52mm | 492mm |
| Non-Boost 15 x 100mm | 44mm | 470mm |
| Non-Boost 15 x 100mm | 52mm | 472mm |

Crown race diameter 1.5". The Fenrir's 110x15 front hub (S1) makes it the Boost version. S1's "490mm … 44mm or 52mm" Frame Specs lines are this fork's numbers.

**Lithic Carbon Mountain Fork** (S6, S7; the fork it ships with now):

- Rear position: 495.0 mm rear axle to crown, 44.0 mm rear offset.
- Front position: 498.0 mm front axle to crown, 51.5 mm front offset.
- S7: "it has an adjustable offset of 7.5mm" (flip chip).
- Steerer Ø28.6 to Ø39.8 crown race, 275.0 mm uncut steerer.

Both flip-chip positions put the axle at almost the same height below the crown at the chart's 68°, so axle height can't tell them apart; only the wheelbase and trail can.

**Wolf Tooth Performance EC headset** (S8): lower assembly "12.25mm stack". Upper "5mm top cover = 16mm total stack".

## Discrepancies

1. **A2C and offset rows (S1/S2/S4) match no fork position.** The geometry table's `495mm` with `51mm` isn't an ENVE position (490/44, 492/52) or a Lithic one (495/44, 498/51.5). It predates the Lithic fork (S4, 2022).
2. **Suspension correction:** 120 mm (S1 Frame Specs) vs 100 mm (S2 image label, S3, S4).
3. **Chainstay positions:** the Geometry table says 430-450mm. The tire-clearance line names 420, 430, and 440 mm positions.
4. **Tuning Chip angle change:** ±0.25° (S1 live) vs ±0.2° (S2, S4).
5. **S wheelbase `1110-1120mm`** is 10 mm wide where every other size is 20 mm wide. It looks like a typo for 1110-1130.
6. **Axle width:** front hub "110x15mm" vs front axle "Ø15x100mm" in the same Frame Specs list.

## Chart consistency

**2026-11-22, Slice 16e.** Committed `TestEveryChartIsConsistent` and `TestBasisSearchCatalogFrames` on the nominal S1 chart (`68°`, `70 mm` drop, `440 mm` CS, `PublishedWheelbaseMm = 0`, HTA half-width `0.5°`, bare head tube):

- **At `495 / 51` (`BasisFork`):** all 5 sizes pass axle height with zero allowances: `XS +1.70 mm` (`±3.81`), `S +2.43 mm` (`±3.84`), `M +1.89 mm` (`±3.91`), `L +2.34 mm` (`±3.97`), `XL +1.80 mm` (`±4.04`); worst excess `-1.41 mm` (`S`).
- **`searchBasis` at `51 mm` offset:** implied A2C `XS 496.84`, `S 497.62`, `M 497.04`, `L 497.53`, `XL 496.94 mm` (range `496.84–497.62 mm`, minimax `497.23 mm`, rounded `497.0 mm`); passing A2C interval `493.50–500.95 mm`; passing offset interval at `497.0 mm` A2C `41.45–59.90 mm`.

**2026-11-21, Slice 16b P2/P3 (prior catalog revision).** The earlier `ea9d423` chart at `495/54` on a bare head tube implied `494.61–495.40 mm` A2C.

**2026-11-21, Slice 16b review** (temporary tests, axle-height budget about ±2.0 mm, wheelbase budget about ±3.0 mm):

1. **The prior `ea9d423` catalog chart fit too well.** At 495/54 on a bare head tube, axle-height mismatch was within 0.4 mm and wheelbase within 0.3 mm in every size. That confirmed its wheelbases were computed from that basis.
2. **S1's nominal chart** (68°, 70 mm drop, 440 mm chainstay, range-midpoint wheelbases) with the Lithic fork on a bare head tube:
   - Axle height passes in every size at both positions: −0.9 to −0.2 mm at 495/44 and at 498/51.5.
   - It passes only up to a 1.2 mm lower cup (0.8 mm at HTA ±0.05).
   - Wheelbase is short of the range midpoints by about 10.5 mm at 51.5 mm and 18.5 mm at 44 mm, in every size.
3. **The same nominal chart with the ENVE Boost fork:**
   - Axle height passes only with an EC44 cup of 3.9–7.4 mm (52 mm position) or 2.6–6.2 mm (44 mm position). At HTA ±0.05 the ranges are 4.2–7.0 mm and 3.0–5.8 mm.
   - The spec'd Wolf Tooth cup is 12.25 mm (S8), so with the shipped headset the chart doesn't fit.
   - Wheelbase is short by about 10.5 mm (52) or 19 mm (44).
4. **Every Tuning Chip pairing** (67.75/68.25° × 68/72 mm × 430/450 mm, endpoint wheelbases) fails the wheelbase check by 7–21 mm against 495/54, 495/44, and 498/51.5.
5. **Trail (hand estimate, not a catalog test):** 88 mm trail at 68° with a 29" wheel matches a 51–52 mm offset, not 44 or 54 mm.

Reading: the chart's front end fits a 495–498 mm fork at about 51 mm offset with no lower cup. That is the chart's own "495mm / 51mm" rows drawn without the headset's lower cup. No Otso wheelbase range fits any basis; they run about 10 mm long.
