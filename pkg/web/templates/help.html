Help and glossary

Comparison specs

Each compared bike is frame[:size][@fork][@sag%]. The form builds these for you; you can also write them straight into the URL as repeated col parameters, and they mean exactly what they do on the command line.

  • fenrir:L@110mm@20% – Otso Fenrir, size L, 110 mm fork at 20% sag.
  • hayduke@rigid – the frame's default size with its rigid fork (no sag).
  • seven-expedition-926f:all@120mm – every size of a frame.
  • Allowed sag is 20%, 25% or 30%. Frames only accept forks inside their supported A2C range.

Example: /?col=fenrir:L@110mm@20%25&col=hayduke:S3@110mm@20%25

Reading the colours

Every scored cell shows its value plus ✓ inside the ideal range, ~ acceptable or ✗ outside, with the distance to the ideal. Sub-scores run from +100 (ideal) down toward per-criterion floors (-100 or -200); a bike's total is their weighted average (> -166 to +100). The ranges below are the default idmatch profile.
